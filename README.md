## 🌐 About Us

AJ Robotics Lab is an independent technology platform focused on exploring modern robotics and engineering technologies.

Our mission is to encourage students, developers, engineers and technology enthusiasts to discover, learn and experiment with innovative technologies.

We believe in practical learning, creative thinking and technological innovation.
